Industrial Organization

Porter's Five Forces — Cloud Industry Structure

Structural analysis explaining why hyperscalers protect margins through asymmetric switching costs and non-compute fees in the Indian cloud market.

Threat: LOW

Threat of New Entrants

Building hyperscale datacenters requires tens of billions in annual CapEx. Securing Nvidia AI accelerators (H100/B200), subsea fiber landing rights in Mumbai, and dedicated power grids creates near-impenetrable barriers.

Power: HIGH

Supplier Power

  • Semiconductors: Heavy dependence on Nvidia, TSMC, Intel/AMD
  • Indian Infra: High industrial electricity tariffs (MSEDCL) and real estate scarcity in Mumbai & Pune
  • Software: High licensing for Windows Server and Oracle stacks
INTENSE OLIGOPOLY

Industry Rivalry

AWS, Azure & GCP hold 77% of Indian enterprise spend. Raw compute prices are transparent and hyper-competitive, pushing providers to extract rents through storage markups, tiered egress, and software bundling.

Compute Margins Compress → Lock-in & Egress Monetize
Power: LOW–MODERATE

Buyer Power

  • Startups & Midsize: Complete price-takers with zero customized discounting power
  • Egress Taxes: Repatriating 5 TB costs ~₹50,000 in exit penalties
  • Proprietary APIs: Migrating off DynamoDB or Entra ID costs millions in refactoring
Threat: LOW

Threat of Substitutes

Private on-prem datacenters require massive upfront CapEx, 3–5 year amortization, and specialized talent. In an era of dynamic peak scaling (Diwali sales, IPL streaming), private infra cannot match hyperscale elasticity.

Porter's Generic Strategies — Hyperscaler Positioning in India
How AWS, Azure, GCP, and Domestic CSPs map to Michael Porter's Cost Leadership, Differentiation, and Focus strategies.
AWS — Cost Leadership via Scale Broad Market

Amazon leverages monumental global infrastructure scale to drive down unit costs. AWS offers the lowest gp3 storage price ($0.0912/GB/mo) and a generous 100 GB/mo free internet egress allowance. Custom in-house silicon (Graviton) delivers industry-leading price-performance ratios.

Microsoft Azure — Differentiation via Ecosystem Bundling Broad Enterprise

Azure monetizes enterprise entrenchment with Microsoft 365, Windows Server, and Active Directory (Entra ID). Offers the lowest 1-year reserved compute rate ($45.50/mo) and Azure Hybrid Benefit discounts, but charges a 45% premium on storage ($0.132/GB) and high egress penalties.

Google Cloud — Differentiation via Open Standards & AI Tech / Data-Driven

Google differentiates on Kubernetes (GKE), Vertex AI, and BigQuery analytics. Google champions open-source portability and became the first hyperscaler to offer zero exit fees for customers migrating away, actively disrupting the AWS-Azure oligopolistic equilibrium.

Domestic CSPs — Focus Strategy on Data Sovereignty Niche / Regulated

CtrlS, Yotta, Tata Communications, and Jio Cloud serve regulated sectors (BFSI, public sector, defense). They capture 23% of Indian spend by guaranteeing strict adherence to the DPDP Act 2023 and RBI data localization rules requiring Indian data to never leave sovereign boundaries.
